# Citation study: current data and comparisons

Generated from committed snapshots; completed outcome years end in 2025.

The roster contains **590 assessments of 551 originals**. 543 originals have verified identities and 543 have publisher dates. Only 35 currently have verified curated challenge dates; these do not yet establish the first assessment of every original.

Article/review citation histories are complete for 21 originals. Cached annual totals across all citing document types are available for 369. These are separate measurements and are never combined within a comparison.

## Cached comparison diagnostic

Same-journal, exact indexed-year controls; up to five donors selected on baseline citations. Missing pool/baseline data block matching. Missing selected follow-up blocks the contrast. Every exclusion remains in the eligibility table.
